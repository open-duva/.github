# DUVA Research

**Collaborative research on AI agents and closed-loop health systems.**

DUVA Research is a collaborative research platform initiated by [DUVA](https://duva.com), focused on AI-driven systems for understanding and supporting human health. We create a shared space for research and contributions across academia and industry, with sleep as our starting point.

Our work connects wearable sensing, multimodal learning, and AI agents to build systems that can perceive human state, coordinate personalized interventions, and adapt through feedback.

## From Sensing to Intervention

We study how wearable intelligence can move beyond measurement and reporting toward timely, personalized action.

At the center of this work is a closed loop:

**Sense → Understand → Act → Learn**

Physiological, behavioral, and environmental signals inform an evolving understanding of the individual. AI agents use this context to select and coordinate interventions, while the observed response informs subsequent decisions.

Our research spans the full loop—from reliable sensing and state estimation to intervention timing, personalization, and evaluation in everyday settings.

## Research Areas

### Health AI Agents & Adaptive Interventions
Context-aware agents that connect physiological understanding with action. We explore personalized decision-making, intervention coordination, and feedback-driven adaptation.

### Multimodal Learning for Human Physiology
Learning from physiological, behavioral, and environmental time series to understand human state. Our interests include representation learning, sensor fusion, and generalization across individuals and contexts.

### Computational Sleep Science
Computational approaches to sleep dynamics, recovery, and their interaction with the environment. We investigate sleep-state estimation, individual variability, and the timing and effects of adaptive interventions.

### Wearable Sensing & Edge Intelligence
Sensing and inference designed for continuous, everyday use. Our interests span low-power signal acquisition, robust sensing in real-world conditions, and efficient on-device AI.

### Acoustic Intelligence & Adaptive Audio
Audio systems that understand and respond to the person and their surroundings. We explore acoustic scene understanding, neural audio processing, adaptive noise control, and personalized sound delivery.

## Collaboration & Research Outputs

We welcome collaboration with researchers, engineers, and institutions working across these areas—from shared methods and experimental systems to joint studies and reproducible evaluation.

As projects mature, we plan to share selected code, datasets, papers, and technical reports. Each release will document its contributors, affiliations, and terms of use.
